About

The short version

I'm a Business Analyst and Project Coordinator based in London, currently at Sainsbury's. Over four years I've worked across retail, logistics, and SME advisory — the common thread is taking a business problem that's vague on day one and turning it into something measurable, documented, and shipped.

That's meant building KPI dashboards stakeholders actually use, cleaning datasets in the tens of thousands of records to find what mattered, redesigning an approval process to cut turnaround by a fifth, and holding transaction accuracy above 99.8% in a high-volume environment.

I'm now working toward Product Management — specifically Associate Product Manager roles in London, ideally in fintech, crypto, or consumer tech. The analyst instinct doesn't go away; it's the same skill aimed one level up, at deciding what gets built and why.
